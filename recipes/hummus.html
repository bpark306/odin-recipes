Hummus

Images from mosthungry.com

Ingredients

  • 1 can of Chickpeas
  • 1/4 cup of lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup of tahini
  • 2 Tbspn of Olive Oil
  • 2 Cloves of Garlic
  • 5 Icecubes

Steps

  1. Blend chickpeas, lemon juice, tahini, olive oil, and garlic in a food proccessor on low
  2. Slowly incorporate ice cubes 1 at a time to make the texture more loose
  3. Stop blending once mixture is completely smooth and salt to taste
  4. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and paprika to desired tatste
