Olive Pasta

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup of Olives
  • 2 Servings of Favorite Pasta
  • 2Tbspn of Olive Oil
  • 2 Garlic Cloves
  • 2 Teaspoon of Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
  • 2Tbspn of Peccorino or Parmeseasn

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to boil
  2. Once a rolling boil has started, cook pasta according to instructions
  3. While pasta is cooking crush the cloves of garlic with the palm of your hand
  4. Once the pasta is done cooking. Set aside a ladle of pasta water for later use. Drain the rest of the pasta and lightly coat the pasta with olive oil
  5. Heat a pan over medium low heat and pour in olive oil. Add the garlic and crushed red pepper flakes frying for 30 seconds.
  6. Add the olives, pasta, and a pasta water and mix.
  7. Once the oilve oil and pasta water has emulsified into a sauce turn off heat and serve
  8. Grate parmesean of peccorino on top to finish
