Pan con Tomate

Image from tastesbetterfromscratch.com

Ingredients

  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 1Tbspn Olive Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Grate the tomates leaving behind the skin
  2. Use a cheesecloth to slightly drain the grated tomato mixture
  3. Mix in 1Tbspn of Olive oil and salt to taste, serve over toast
